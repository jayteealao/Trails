fix(android): keep unsent changes safe on clear, stop repeat uploads and fill in article rows

- Clear data and account switch now recount after the backup push and ask
  before wiping when articles skipped after repeated failures (or edits made
  during the push) are still only on the device.
- A pushed article is stamped with a time taken before it was read, so an
  edit made while the upload is in flight still counts as unsynced; a retried
  run skips rows an earlier run already sent, and a run stopped part-way now
  reports an error instead of success.
- Rows deleted before backups were stamped are now sent by the never-backed-up
  sweep, so they no longer hold the unsynced count up forever.
- The one-time restore gives up after five attempts instead of retrying an
  article that never applies forever.
- Clearing data also empties the image cache; the archive and image cache
  folder names live in one place.
- Restore reads image, author and video rows only for articles the remote side
  writes, with the reads in parallel; video rows are now backed up and restored
  like images and authors; image, author and video rows are indexed by article.
- Recently saved articles get their lead image and byline rows from a
  background pass after each sync, without being opened first.
- Settings: a failed Retry is shown to the person, Retry stays focusable and
  reads as "Retrying", the failure count is announced, focus moves to Sync Now
  when the row goes away, the skip count comes from the limit itself, and the
  counts are bold; "More than 999 changes" is lower case mid-sentence.
- README describes the FTS5 search.
